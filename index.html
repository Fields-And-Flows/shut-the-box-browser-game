LEARN THE GAME

A road to victory

01 · Build a road

Connect opposite edges with your visible flat stones and capstones. Roads turn at right angles, never diagonally. Either direction wins. If a move creates roads for both players, the mover wins.

02 · The opening exchange

White goes first. On each player's first turn, place one of your opponent's flat stones on an empty square. After these two turns, place your own pieces or move a stack.

03 · Three kinds of pieces

Flats build roads. Walls block roads and cannot be covered by ordinary stones. Capstones build roads, cannot be covered, and can flatten a wall when dropped alone as the final piece of a move.

04 · Carry and spread

Control a stack with your color on top. Carry any number of its top pieces up to the board's width, keeping their order. Move in one straight orthogonal direction, dropping at least one piece on every square, bottom first. Walls can move too.

05 · A flat finish

If the board fills or either player has no stones and no capstones left in reserve, the game ends. Roads take priority; otherwise, most visible flat stones wins. Walls, capstones, and buried pieces don't score. Equal scores draw.

06 · Make yourself at home

Play with a friend on the same device. Click or tap squares; keyboard players can Tab to squares and use Enter or Space. Undo takes back a completed turn. Changing board size takes effect when you start a new game.
